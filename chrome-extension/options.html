Kety for Google Meet

This extension listens to live captions in Google Meet and keeps the conversation on this computer. When you’re finished, it sends the text to the Kety app- automatically if you turned that on in Kety, or when you tap Sync with app. Summaries you write in Kety stay in Kety; this is the raw meeting text.

Link to Kety

In Kety open Settings → Captures → Chrome extension and copy the code here-or install from the ZIP Kety gives you; it fills this in for you.

Only change this if Kety told you to. Most people keep the default.

Send to Kety

While Meet is open, your words are saved here. When you close the Meet tab, Kety can pick it up right away if you enabled auto-send in the Kety app-otherwise use Sync with app below. The Kety app must be open on this computer.

Recent meetings (24 hours)

Each row is one Meet. After you leave the Meet tab, you can send it to Kety with Sync with app. Copy grabs the full text. Nothing leaves this browser until you choose.

Meetings disappear from this list 24 hours after the last captions update. Closing the Meet tab does not erase the row-it simply lets you send to Kety.
